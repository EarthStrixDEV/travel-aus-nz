Practical tips

Cards, passes & access

Transit cards

Sydney: Opal, or tap a contactless card. Melbourne: myki. Brisbane: go card or contactless. Auckland: AT HOP or contactless. Wellington: Snapper on Metlink buses.

Passes & caps

Daily and weekly fare caps apply on most city networks, so a day of hopping rarely costs more than one cap. Book long-distance rail and Interislander sailings ahead in summer.

Accessibility

Major city stations are largely step-free; check each operator's station guide before you go. Ferries at main wharves have ramp access, and bus drivers can deploy a ramp on request.
