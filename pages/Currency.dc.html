Money tips

Paying, calmly

Tap almost everywhere

Cards and phone wallets are accepted nearly everywhere, from cafés to city transit.

Cash is rarely needed

Carry a little for markets and remote areas. Some cafés now refuse cash entirely.

Tipping isn't expected

Staff earn a full wage. Rounding up for great service is welcome, never required.

Check foreign fees

Many cards add 2–3% per purchase. When asked, always pay in the local currency.

Use ATMs inside banks

Bank-branch ATMs are safer and usually cheaper than standalone machines.

GST refund, Australia only

Spend A$300+ with one shop within 60 days of departure, then claim via the TRS at the airport.
